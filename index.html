What we found

The DC car service and airport pages were rewritten recently, they answer real traveler questions, and the reviews on them are fresh and specific. The content is not the gap. The gaps are in how the pages connect to each other and how Google reads the business as a local entity.

Several pages compete for the same searches

High impact

When two or more pages target the same phrase, Google has to guess which one should rank, so each one ranks lower than it could. The corporate side also has a link labelled "Executive Car Service" that redirects back to the main car service page, while a separate executive page exists.
